Research agenda

AI that can see, reason, and earn trust.

My work connects medical imaging, language, and domain knowledge—moving from capable models toward systems that are clinically useful, interpretable, and carefully evaluated.

01

Multimodal clinical intelligence

Combining CT, chest X-rays, longitudinal patient records, and free-text reports so models can reason across the same evidence clinicians use.

  • CT & radiography
  • Vision-language models
  • Longitudinal data
02

Trustworthy evaluation

Building expert-aligned ways to assess AI-generated radiology reports and multimodal model outputs beyond surface-level text similarity.

  • Radiologist feedback
  • LLM evaluation
03

Biomedical language agents

Connecting language models with clinical calculators and curated scientific databases to make their reasoning more verifiable and useful.

  • Tool learning
  • Self-verification
